# Overview

Peeps is a social media platform for friends and family to share their lives. Unlike Facebook and other platforms, peeps is designed to be centered around the family unit.  Feeds and posts are all derived from direct connections, not randomized algorithms, machine learning, or marketing strategies.

The main landing page for peeps is *your* page. Anybody can creeate a famiily page, but you can only join a page by confirming a relationship with an existing family member. It's almost like SAP or Salesforce, but for families. It can be used for other groups as well, friend groups, clubs, etc, but the primary focus is on family.

Another focal point is communications. Today's family are often widespread, spanning multiple timezones and countries, and where each person has their own preferred method of communication. Peeps seeks to be the central communication hub, tracking anf facilitating threads and contacts across many pipelines. Whether it's email, SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebbok Messenger, or any other method, peeps will help you stay connected and included in the conversation.

The last major focal point is events. Birthdays, holidays, and other special occasions that require logistics and organization and communicating across a large group of people.
